Inside the model

How the AI works

1. Every film becomes a vector

Each feature is a column: 1 if the film has it, 0 if not, multiplied by its weight. Orange's Cosine distance then measures the angle between two films' vectors.

similarity(A, B) = (A · B) / (|A| × |B|) → 100% = identical, 0% = nothing shared
Feature weights (data/model_settings.toml)

2. Similarity map

Brighter squares mean more similar films. Hover or tap a square to read it.

3. Movie families (Orange hierarchical clustering)

Orange grouped films whose vectors sit close together. Each family is named after its most common mood and genres.
